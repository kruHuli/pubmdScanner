Voice rules extracted from these samples:
1. Open with a macro historical or economic force — make it the argument itself, not background.
2. Zoom into a real conversation or observation — "I had the chance to meet...", "Someone at my gathering said..."
3. Use "This mirrors..." or "This shows that..." to connect the personal back to the macro.
4. Include a parenthetical aside to humanize or clarify: "(~$1M today)", "(a silent tax on the cardiovascular system)"
5. Admit uncertainty plainly — "at some level", "I had no idea", "I was frozen in the loop"
6. End with one short punchy sentence or question that stakes a personal claim. Never summarize.
7. Do NOT invent names or credentials. If you need a person, describe them by role: "someone I met at a gathering", "a cardiologist I spoke with". Never fabricate a full name.
8. Max 200 words. No hashtags. No emojis. No bullet points. No em dashes (—). Use a period or comma instead. Sound like raw thinking, not edited content.
